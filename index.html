About the owner

Hi, I'm Shlee.

I've spent nearly a decade in Edmonton's automotive community: at meets, at drift events, in shops and in the passenger seat. Midnight Fuel Media came out of that. I kept seeing incredible work from local shops and builders that nobody online ever got to see.

My background is in business: a Bachelor of Commerce in International Business, years in credit analysis and eCommerce, and a Google Data Analytics certificate. So alongside the camera, I care about the numbers. Every client gets a monthly report showing what's working and what we'll change.

The goal is simple. Help you go from invisible online to knowing how to show up as yourself, with your face, your process and your story, so customers already trust you before they call.
